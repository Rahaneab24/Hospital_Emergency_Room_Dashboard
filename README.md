# 🏥 Hospital_Emergency_Room_Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyses 19 months of emergency room data (Apr 2023 – Oct 2024) to uncover patterns in patient wait times, satisfaction, referrals, peak hours and demographics, helping hospital management improve staffing and patient flow.

## 📖 Project Overview

Emergency rooms operate under constant pressure: unpredictable volumes, limited staff and patients who need care quickly. This project turns raw ER records into a clear, navigable dashboard that answers questions such as:

How long do patients wait, and how satisfied are they?<br>
Which days and hours are the busiest?<br>
Which departments receive the most referrals?<br>
Who are the patients (age and race distribution)?<br>
How many patients are admitted versus treated and released?<br>

## 🎯 Business Problem

Hospital management needs data-driven answers to allocate staff, reduce wait times and improve patient experience. This dashboard provides a single place to monitor ER performance and spot where operations can improve.

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

The workbook has four navigable views, with a menu panel for quick switching:

Monthly View – month-by-month trends in ER activity.<br>
Consolidated View – overall KPIs and summary visuals across the whole period.<br>
Patient Details – drill-down into individual patient records.<br>
Key Takeaways – written descriptive analysis and conclusions (shown below).<br>

## 🔍 Key Insights
####  ⏱ Wait Time & Satisfaction
Average wait time: 35.3 minutes, indicating room to improve patient flow.
Average satisfaction score: 4.99 / 10, showing moderate satisfaction.

#### 🔁 Departmental Referrals
5,400 patients did not need a referral.
Among those referred: General Practice (1,840), Orthopedics (995), Physiotherapy (276) and Cardiology (248).

#### 📅 Peak Busy Periods
Busiest days: Monday (1,377), Saturday (1,322), Tuesday (1,318).
Busiest hours: 11 AM, 7 PM, 1 PM and 11 PM.

#### 👥 Patient Demographics
Age: 30–39 is the largest group (1,200), followed by 20–29 (1,188). Middle-aged patients (40–50) also form a significant share.<br>
Race: White (2,571), African American (1,951), Multi-racial (1,557), Asian (1,060); 1,030 patients declined to identify.<br>

#### 🏨 Admission Patterns
Admitted: 4,612 | Treated and released: 4,604 – a nearly even split.

## 💡 Recommendations
Staff for peaks: increase coverage on Mondays and during the 11 AM, 1 PM, 7 PM and 11 PM windows.<br>
Cut wait times: review triage and patient flow, especially in busy periods, since wait time likely affects the low satisfaction score.<br>
Strengthen General Practice and Orthopedics capacity, as they receive the most referrals.<br>
Plan resources for a diverse population, including patient communication and support services.<br>
